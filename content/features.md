## Judgment over counts

Other tools count what happened. Spindle checks it against what was possible. A red flag means something went wrong **and** the player could have stopped it.

## What it checks

- **Interrupts and stops.** Must-kick casts that finished while a player's interrupt was off cooldown, compared with how many they took. Crowd control that lands mid-cast counts as a stop. Pet interrupts count for their owner.
- **Major cooldowns.** How much of a pull a player's big cooldowns sat ready.
- **Group defensives.** Heavy group damage with defensives still available.
- **Tank-busters.** Boss hits on the tank with no mitigation.
- **Healer cooldowns.** Used when the group needed them, or held through the moment.
- **Deaths.** The cause, and whether a defensive was ready.

## Your view and the group's

Each player gets their own view. A key leader sees the whole party on one board.
